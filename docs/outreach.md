# Creator outreach checklist

The current public artifact is enough for static RE, but creator material could
preserve content that is otherwise at risk. The highest-value request is for
permission to archive any surviving material privately while attribution and
release terms are discussed.

Ask whether they retain any of the following:

- Xcode project/source, including the internal `shogun` project
- Release, debug, ad-hoc, or development builds
- Original encrypted App Store IPAs
- Decrypted/internal IPAs or loose `.app` bundles
- Version 1.1.0 or any other historical release
- `Hills and Rivers Remain Lite` / `国破れて山河あり Lite` (App Store ID
  `338528080`), which contains original scenarios not present in the full game
- Build scripts, third-party library versions, map tools, and asset converters
- Original art/audio/source assets and specifications for DAT, YAS, and PVR data
- Design documents, version history, server/map-download data, and test content
- Approval for preservation, documentation, and/or a native reimplementation

Useful identifiers to include:

- Full app ID: `334825813`
- Lite app ID: `338528080`
- Bundle ID: `com.square-enix.HillsAndRiversRemain`
- Japanese title: `国破れて山河あり`
- Known scene filename: `Hills and Rivers Remain 1.1.0-H.ipa`

## Short English draft

> I am preserving and documenting the discontinued 2009 iPhone game *Hills and
> Rivers Remain* (`国破れて山河あり`). We have recovered a decrypted 2.0.0
> build, but older versions and the unique Lite scenarios remain at risk. Do you
> have any surviving source, Xcode projects, builds, assets, tools, documents, or
> server/map data? We would be glad to preserve them privately and discuss your
> preferred terms before publishing anything. Our goal is historical
> preservation and, with permission, a faithful native reimplementation.
